Summary

The inpatient admission of your patient took place for the planned 9th cycle of chemotherapy with carboplatin. In an externally performed CT scan (head to pelvis) dated 08/18/2024, there appeared to be a suspicious cerebellar metastasis on the right. In addition, pronounced leukocyturia was noted in the urine, so chemotherapy was initially withheld, and an MRI of the head along with antibiotic therapy using Cipro was performed. MRI of the head ruled out cerebral metastasis.
On 09/09/2024, the patient received chemotherapy with carboplatin (200 milligrams) along with appropriate antiemetic therapy. The chemotherapy was well-tolerated by the patient.
As Mr. W. became increasingly disoriented to place and situation, a neurology consult as well as a psychiatry consult were called several times (see findings below). Therapy with Risperdal was initiated, and a follow-up visit in the Memory Clinic was scheduled.
The patient was able to be discharged home on 09/10/2024 after a final blood count check and in good general condition.
