Summary and assessment:
Current MIBG scan shows, in correlation with the suspicious recurrence finding on left-sided retroperitoneal MRI, pathological MIBG uptake. Overall, the findings were interpreted as locoregional recurrence of ganglioneuroblastoma. Bone marrow examinations showed no evidence of bone marrow involvement.

Treatment plan is as follows: After placement of a Broviac catheter, a neoadjuvant chemotherapy cycle N8 (per Neuroblastoma Protocol 2004 High Risk with topotecan, cyclophosphamide, etoposide) is to be administered. This will be followed by tumor biopsy for histological confirmation and evaluation of "druggable targets." Complete tumor resection will most likely not be possible. After a second neuroblastoma cycle, repeat staging studies are planned. If there is a response to therapy, two additional N8 cycles are recommended, followed by high-dose chemotherapy (conditioning with, for example, melphalan, etoposide, carboplatin) with autologous stem cell rescue, possibly in combination with an MIBG therapy. For high-dose therapy, prior collection of autologous stem cells is required (stem cell apheresis, for instance, after 1st block N8). As maintenance therapy, monoclonal antibody therapy (anti-GD2 antibody ch14.18) may be considered.
