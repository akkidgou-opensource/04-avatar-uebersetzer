Summary and Assessment:
The patient presented on 11/05/51 with hepatic encephalopathy grade I–II and tense ascites due to renewed decompensation of previously known cryptogenic liver cirrhosis, Child Class C, in our emergency room. In the absence of clinical or historical evidence for an inciting cause, and after ruling out significant transjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt dysfunction, potential triggers for the decompensation to consider include:
a) unclear infection (supported by moderate inflammatory lab findings),
b) possible worsening of pre-existing vasodilation with progressive hypotension (dizziness, BP 100/60 mmHg) possibly due to ACE inhibitor therapy.

Rapid and satisfactory recompensation was achieved with administration of lactulose enemas, oral lactulose, temporary protein restriction to 40 grams per day (supplemented with essential amino acids), additional ornithine aspartate, intensification of diuretic combination therapy (spironolactone + torsemide), and initial total paracentesis. As you are already aware, continued stool regulation with lactulose as needed, targeting 2–4 bowel movements/day, is recommended. Additionally, diuretic therapy should be titrated under close body weight monitoring (e.g., daily).
